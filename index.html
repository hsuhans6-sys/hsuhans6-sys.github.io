01 / Introduction

About me

My engineering experience includes CAD modelling, mechanical assemblies, engineering drawings and hands-on robot construction. University projects and industry placements have helped me understand how an initial concept becomes a practical mechanism.

I am now developing my programming and robotics skills, building on my background in mechanical engineering.

Career & research interests

I am interested in robotic manipulation, assistive devices and mechanical design for robotic systems. My aim is to combine practical hardware design with sensing and control in future engineering development work.

A mechanical design project exploring an affordable device to support rehabilitation exercises for stroke patients.

Solid Edge · KeyShot · Mechanical Design

A mechanism design project combining cam-profile calculation, motion simulation and mechanical assembly design.
